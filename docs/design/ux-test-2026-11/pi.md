# PI report: player input methods, touch/phone, keyboard, gamepad, accessibility (as returned by the tester)

17 findings: S1 x1, S2 x8, S3 x8. Scripts/screenshots in scratchpad/ux/pi (OUT below).

## Scope
- Projects: `sample` (1920x1080) and `fresh` with `template: 'portrait'` (1080x1920; script/start.vn replaced by a longer one with long line, menu, `input`, sound cue). A third private `sample` copy (`export-motion`) got rain, shake, flash, wipe, camera shake, wavy text and sound cues.
- Method: touch and most keyboard/pad work ran against the web export (`tools/export-web.mjs --no-build --no-playtest`, own static server, headless Edge). The dev page has a 44 px dev bar and no rotate cover.
- Viewports: phone landscape 844x390, portrait 390x844 (DPR 3, isMobile, hasTouch); tablet 1024x768, 768x1024; desktop 1280x800, 1280x720. Safe areas via CDP Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride.
- Not covered: real phone, real screen reader (accessibility tree via ariaSnapshot only), real/non-standard gamepad, demo/tutorial projects, virtual keyboard for `input` on touch, audio, real browser zoom.
- Pinch zoom and pull-to-refresh inconclusive (CDP synthesizePinchGesture bypasses touch-action; two-finger spread via dispatchTouchEvent did nothing; double-tap never zoomed; html/body have overscroll-behavior none, stage touch-action none). Needs a real device; no finding filed.

## Findings
PI-1 · S1 · bug · Portrait template on a phone: Load/Save and Options overflow the stage, Back is off-screen. Steps: `vn init --template portrait`, export, open on a portrait phone (390x844, hasTouch). On the title tap Load (or play, long-press, Save; or Options). Scripts t1h-portrait-load.mjs, t1g-portrait-overflow.mjs, t1-flow.mjs phoneP portrait. Stock screens are laid out for 1920 px. On Load the right-column slots and Back sit at x=560-632 in a 390 px window. Two-finger tap and long press do nothing there, so only a page reload gets out. 24 of 53 elements overflow, worst by 263 px. On Options sliders, % values and Accessibility tab content are clipped on the right. Evidence shots/portrait-01-portrait-load.png, portrait-02-phoneP-options.png. Reproduced twice. Known: cause yes (TODO.md "Configurable stage size", docs/game-json.md "supply screen title, screen options..."); the consequence (a template that ships a trap on phones) is not stated.

PI-2 · S2 · usability · Phone landscape: all stock UI text except dialogue is tiny. Sample export at 844x390, font-size x stage scale (t1c-fontsizes.mjs, captions on). Dialogue 16.0 (floor works); name tag 12.3; options labels 12.3, option buttons and tabs 14.4; save slot title 10.1, "Empty" 11.6; history speaker 9.4; toast 10.1; sound caption 9.4; quick bar labels 8.7. The bar is boosted in height but not in text. Expected a floor (about 12-14 px) for bars, captions, toasts; captions are an accessibility feature and are the least readable text. Evidence shots/motion-01-phoneL-caption.png, sample-01-phoneL-sample-line1.png. Reproduced twice. Known: partly (mobile.md step 3 names only the dialogue floor and bar height).

PI-3 · S2 · usability · Phone landscape: most targets under 44 CSS px; a tap beside the quick bar advances the story. Measured at 844x390 (scale 0.36): quick bar 26.4 px tall (mobile.md promises 32, boost capped by room); title and pause buttons 152x30; choices 361x38; Options tabs 87-130x23; sliders and switches 224x22; Fullscreen and Back 23 px tall; Save/Load page tabs 23x20; History Back 72x23. Tablet landscape fine (45/55/32/32-34). Portrait template on 390x693: choices 46, quick bar 29, title 30. A tap 12 px above "History" advanced the line instead of pressing it (near-miss help applies only to open screens and menus). Near-miss on title and choices worked. Evidence t1-flow.mjs phoneL sample, t1f-misses.mjs, shots/sample-02-phoneL-options.png, sample-03-phoneL-save.png. Reproduced twice. Known: mostly (mobile.md step 3; Problems check open in TODO.md).

PI-4 · S2 · a11y · Quick bar has no backing: fails contrast on light scenes and overlaps the text box edge. Label colour rgba(230,233,239,.7), no background/border/shadow. Computed contrast against the scene (t7c-calc.mjs): cream sky 1.02:1, white 1.15, the sample's sunset glow 1.36, mid-grey path 3.0, the sample's grass 3.8 (WCAG wants 4.5). Private export with a cream bg_park_evening.png (t7c-contrast.mjs, shots/bright-01-bright-quickbar.png; the in-script pixel comparison was disabled by a late edit, numbers from the formula). On the phone the bar's top (y=358) is 12 px above the box bottom (y=370), so labels straddle the box's lower border; it also overlaps the Save panel edge (sample-03-phoneL-save.png). Contrast computed once; overlap reproduced twice. Known: no.

PI-5 · S2 · a11y · Screen reader: Save/Load slot buttons have no accessible name; delete is "x"; page tabs are "1".."9". Open Save or Load, `locator('#savesui').ariaSnapshot()` (t7-sr.mjs, t7b-sr2.mjs). `- button` with no name, then sibling text `Empty Slot 1`. With a save: `- button`, `- text: "Slot 4 11/8/26, 1:41 PM Alice: ..."`, `- button "×"`. Tabbing a slot announces just "button". Tabs read `button "1"`..`"9"` (and A/Q/C on Load) without "page". Expected "Slot 4, date, last line", "Delete slot 4", "Page 1". Reproduced twice. Known: no.

PI-6 · S2 · a11y · Screen reader: Options sliders named by setting keys with raw values; switches bare "On"/"Off". (t7b-sr2.mjs) `slider "master": "0.8"`, `"text size": "1"`, `"box opacity": "1"`. aria-label comes from data-setting (lower case, not the visible "Master volume"), no aria-valuetext so no "80%". Switches (Text sounds, Reduce motion, Sound captions, Screen reader reads dialogue, Touch gestures) are `button "On"`/`"Off"`; the label is a separate text node, no aria-pressed or role=switch. Selects read "Normal ▾". Tabs have role=tab but no tablist or tabpanel. Expected name = visible label, value text with unit, switch semantics. Reproduced twice. Known: no (TODO.md says only "no localized label" for the SR option).

PI-7 · S2 · a11y · Keyboard: focus dropped after a Save/Load page tab and after saving; no save confirmation; closing a screen returns focus to the first button. (t3b-focusring.mjs, t3-keyboard.mjs) Esc, Down, Enter (Save); Right (tab 2), Enter: activeElement is BODY (page switched, keyboard lost, next arrow restarts at tab "1"). Arrow to a slot, Enter: it saves, focus is BODY, toast still shows old "Quick saved" (no "Saved" feedback, no live announcement). Open Options from the title with Enter, then Esc: focus goes to New game, not Options (same for Load). Expected focus stays or returns to the opener; a "Saved to slot 4" status toast. Reproduced twice. Known: partly (TODO.md: "when a screen closes with focus on a button, focus falls to the page", framed as Narrator); page-tab and save cases are new.

PI-8 · S2 · usability · Keyboard: Tab does nothing in narration (it is Skip), quick bar unreachable by Tab, no key help anywhere in the game. New game, Tab 3 times on unread text (t3c-keys2.mjs, t3-keyboard.mjs): focus stays on BODY, text does not move (Skip passes only read text). Tab reaches the quick bar only while a choice is up. The working keys (L, A, H, F5/F9, V, Esc, Page Up, number keys, arrows) appear only in docs/game-shell.md; title, pause menu and Options do not show them. Expected Tab moves focus, or Skip is discoverable; a Controls entry (keyboard, pad, touch). Reproduced twice. Known: no.

PI-9 · S2 · a11y · Stock menu focus indicator is thin and low-contrast; quick bar's is almost invisible. (t3b-focusring.mjs) Title, pause, options and save buttons change only the border colour: 2 stage px (1.3 CSS px at 1280 wide, ~0.7 on phone landscape), white 25 % to #5fd1c3. Focused vs unfocused border contrast 1.84:1 (WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1). Quick bar focus is an 8 % white wash plus label 70 % to full opacity, no border or outline (shots/sample-01-kb-quickbar-focus.png; title ring sample-01-kb-title-tab1.png). Choices and sliders get a 4 px accent outline, so the set is inconsistent. Expected 4 px outline everywhere. Reproduced twice. Known: no.

PI-10 · S3 · usability · Touch gestures are not discoverable. All work (t2-gestures.mjs). PI-16 · S3 · docs · Captions: loops and music not captioned; docs promise text for cues without a file. Seen once; may be dev-preview only. t8-captions.mjs on|off. Known: the loop/music limit is documented. Label: taste/docs.

## What worked well
- Gestures: all five work first time with sensible thresholds; never clash with the History list or Options sliders. touch-action and overscroll-behavior keep pull-to-refresh out. Near-miss taps on title buttons, choices and open menus land on the right control.
- Dialogue text floor (16 CSS px) holds on a phone; Options go up to 150 %.
- Reduced motion is on by default when the OS asks, toggles both ways, and really stops rain, shake, flash, camera shake, wavy text and bursts. Wipes become a dissolve.
- Screen reader dialogue: polite live region speaks each line once, after it is fully shown, as `Speaker: text`, clears between lines. Quick save has a role=status toast; captions are a live region too.
- Keyboard and pad menus: arrows, d-pad and stick move spatially; Enter, Space and A press; Esc and B go back; LB/RB switch Options tabs; number keys pick choices; X/Y work on the OSK. Overwrite and Main-menu confirms default to "No".
- Forced colours (emulated) leaves every control legible; light colour scheme changes nothing.
- The rotate cover appears in the right orientations and goes away on rotating back; the game keeps its place.
- Tablet landscape gets 45 px buttons because buttons scale with the stage. The portrait template fills the width of a portrait phone.
